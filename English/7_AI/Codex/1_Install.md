### 2. Using CC-Switch to connect domestic LLM to Codex

Without a ChatGPT account, you can still run Codex by connecting to domestic models like Deepseek or Zhipu GLM.

#### What is CC-switch

> It runs a proxy on your local machine, intercepts all requests from the Codex App to OpenAI, redirects them to your designated model vendor, and keeps the Codex App completely unaware, believing it's chatting with OpenAI.
